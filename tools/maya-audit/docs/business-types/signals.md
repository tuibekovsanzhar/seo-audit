**How a page gets a type.** Points per kind of signal, each kind once: address word 4, structured-data type 3, a word in the title or H1 2, a word in the menu or link text pointing to the page 2, a content signal 1. A type needs 3; ties go to the higher rank. Without a match the general page type (Stage 3) maps to the profile's type marked "Genel sayfa türü", else "other" (weight 25). The homepage, search/cart/account pages (0) and policy pages (15) are typed first. A structured-data type on half or more of the pages read is a site default and is ignored.

**Address words:** `word` anywhere in a segment; `word/*` a folder with an item after it (a detail page); `word/` the last segment (the list page itself). Language folders (`/en/`) are skipped.

**Weights:** by rank 100 / 85 / 70 / 55 / 40 / 30; the homepage is at least 85; "eklendi" = added by Stage 4 (the homepage where the sector list has none, at 85; "Rehber / SSS / blog" at 30, citable).

**Durum** (what to review): **tahmin** = drafted, not yet seen matching a page on a saved audit; **görüldü** = it matched pages on the saved audits named (nihan, moodist, enpara, adile; offline, 2026-11-05). Seen is not proof that the match is right.

**Content signals:** `form` a form that is not search, sign-in, cart or newsletter; `price` price markup (Offer, PriceSpecification) or a price word in a heading; `map` an embedded map or GeoCoordinates; `calculator` a 'hesapla' / 'calculate' button or heading; `faq` FAQPage markup or three or more question headings; `table` a data table; `list` the general page type is a listing (category).
